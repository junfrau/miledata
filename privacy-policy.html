Privacy Policy for MileRich

Effective date: October 12, 2026

MileRich ("the App") is a credit-card signup-bonus tracker and recommender. This policy explains what data the App handles. The App does not require an account, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Data stored on your device

The following data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Cards you add to your wallet (card names, issuers, balances, notes)
  • Your language preference (English / Korean)
  • A cached copy of the public card-bonus database

This data is stored using the device's standard app storage and is removed if you uninstall the App.

2. Card bonus database

The App downloads its credit-card bonus data from a public data repository maintained by the developer. These downloads contain no information about you; the App sends no personal data in return.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, financial account numbers, precise location, or contacts. The App has no analytics, crash-reporting, or push-notification services that transmit your data.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described in Section 3 (ads served by Google AdMob).

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
